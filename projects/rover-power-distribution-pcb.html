Rover Power Distribution PCB

More power, better thermals, and a fuse that tells you it has blown.

WhereUCSD Yonder Dynamics
When2025
ToolsAltium Designer
Spec24 V / 60 A, two layers
Outcome20% smaller footprint; deployed on the competition rover

The rover’s power distribution board needed a connector refresh, so while we were at it we took the opportunity to fix a couple of things that had been bothering us.

I rebuilt it as a custom two-layer schematic and layout in Altium, rerouting around the new connectors and cleaning up the architecture. For thermal headroom at worst-case load I used polygon pours around the XF vias and scaled trace widths to each channel’s current draw. I also wired an indicator LED in parallel with every fuse, so a blown fuse announces itself instead of having to be probed for.

I populated and soldered the board when it arrived and ran an electrical stress test on the traces; it handled around 860 W before they burned out. The redesign cut the PCB footprint by 20% and was deployed on last year’s competition rover.
