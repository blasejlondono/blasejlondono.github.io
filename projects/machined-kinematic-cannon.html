Machined Kinematic Cannon

A fun introduction to manual and CNC machining.

WhereMachining seminar, UC San Diego
WhenMarch – May 2026
ToolsManual mill and lathe, CNC mill and lathe, Fusion 360
Tolerance±0.003″ across all mating features

I machined and assembled this kinematic ball bearing launcher for a machining seminar at UC San Diego. The drawings and the Fusion 360 assembly were pre-designed; what I took away from it was the fundamentals of manual and CNC milling and turning, along with a first working sense of GD&T, DFM and tolerance stacking.

The cannon is manually-milled aluminium base plates and struts, a manually-lathed aluminium firing pin, a CNC-milled Delrin chassis and ball bearing tray, and a CNC-lathed nozzle. Every component was machined to ±0.003″, and it initially passed a surface finish inspection — I have since dropped it once and spilled oil on it, which the finish records faithfully.
