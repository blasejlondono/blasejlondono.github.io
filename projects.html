Internships & research

  • NFAC Transducer Adaptor PCB

    A six-year-old facility worknote described a signal-clipping condition on NFAC’s strain gauge amplifiers. I designed a small KiCad adaptor that ties excitation common to the negative signal leg and satisfies the amplifier’s 10 V window — drawn entirely on airgapped machines, with every footprint built by hand from datasheets.

    KiCad · NASA Ames Research Center · 2026

  • Motor Control Architecture

    Our five-bar legged testbed was talking to its motor controllers over USB, with enough timing jitter to limit how tightly we could close a loop. I moved it to a CAN bus on a Raspberry Pi with ODrive controllers and absolute encoder feedback.

    CAN bus · ODrive · Gravish Lab · 2026

UCSD Yonder Dynamics — Mars rover

  • Rover Fin-Ray End Effector

    Competition rules change every year, so the manipulator has to grab objects we cannot specify in advance. A fin-ray gripper printed in TPU buckles its ribs in a controlled way and conforms to whatever it touches. It went to competition on the rover.

    TPU · ASA · Compliant mechanisms · 2026

  • Carbon Fiber Wheel Hubs

    The rover was running close to the competition weight limit, and the high-infill PLA hubs were the easiest thing to take weight out of. Wood-lathed mould, three-ply vacuum-bag wet layup, CNC post-processing — 50% lighter than the originals.

    Composites · Onshape · Tormach PCNC 440 · 2025

  • Rover Power Distribution PCB

    A 24 V / 60 A two-layer board rebuilt in Altium around new connectors, with trace widths scaled per channel, polygon pours for thermal headroom, and an indicator LED across every fuse so a blown fuse announces itself. 20% smaller footprint.

    Altium Designer · Power electronics · 2025

Projects & coursework

  • Thermoacoustic Refrigerator

    A standing wave in a sealed tube drives a pressure gradient across a stack and pumps heat — a refrigeration cycle with no compressor and no refrigerant. I led a three-person IEEE-HKN team, found the resonator’s natural frequency in ANSYS Fluent, and got a steady 4°C drop.

    ANSYS Fluent · Onshape · IEEE-HKN · 2026

  • Machined Kinematic Cannon

    A spring-loaded ball bearing launcher machined from supplied drawings for a UCSD machining seminar. Manual mill and lathe for the aluminium, CNC for the Delrin chassis and nozzle, every mating feature held to ±0.003″.

    Manual mill & lathe · CNC · GD&T · 2026

  • Pirouette Recognition LSTM

    An LSTM in MATLAB classifying turn type from IMU time series, trained on a public dataset of about 85 labelled turns across six dancers, with joint torques backed out from inertia estimates fed in alongside the raw signal.

    MATLAB · Deep learning · IMU · 2025

  • MAE3 Robotic Arm

    Start inside a 10″ cube, then roughly triple your height to put rings on a 3 ft model of the Sun God. I led the mechanical design around a 2:1 rack and pinion packed along the cube’s diagonal, reaching 28″ of vertical extension under load.

    Fusion 360 · Laser cutting · Sheet metal · 2025

  • MAE3 Pendulum Clock

    An escapement-wheel clock with a deliberately irregular (monkey-shaped) pendulum. A MATLAB optimisation placed eight adjustable weights to align the mass centre and hold a two-second period, landing inside 2.8% timing error.

    MATLAB · Laser cutting · Dynamics · 2025
